CAPABILITIES

End-to-end engineering for constrained systems

Vortiq combines domain expertise across electronics, embedded software, perception and control to deliver practical systems. Our work focuses on measurable outcomes: reduced technical risk, reproducible performance and clear paths to deployment.

Sensor & Signal Chain

Analog front-ends, calibration and robust sensing strategies tailored to the environment.

Embedded Firmware

Deterministic, testable firmware with diagnostics, OTA update paths and clear state machines.

Perception & ML

Edge-aware models, quantization and validation on representative datasets.

Control & Estimation

State estimation, robust controller design and hardware-in-the-loop validation.

Connectivity & Operations

Telemetry, secure OTA and pragmatic edge/cloud trade-offs for monitoring and updates.

Power & Thermal

Power budgeting, energy-aware sensing and thermal design for long-term reliability.

APPROACH

How Vortiq reduces risk on complex projects

Complex systems fail when interfaces are unclear and assumptions go untested. Vortiq's process is evidence-driven: we define measurable hypotheses, run focused experiments, and translate validated results into engineering backlogs with clear acceptance criteria.

Hypothesis-driven prototyping

Small experiments that isolate risk — sensor choice, model performance, or thermal behaviour — before committing to full system designs.

Engineering transfer

Research outputs are integrated into firmware and hardware with automated tests and hardware-in-the-loop checks to maintain performance during scaling.
