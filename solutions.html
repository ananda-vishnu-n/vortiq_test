APPROACH

How Vortiq reduces risk on complex projects

Complex systems fail when interfaces are unclear and assumptions go untested. Vortiq's process is evidence-driven: we define measurable hypotheses, run focused experiments, and translate validated results into engineering backlogs with clear acceptance criteria.

Hypothesis-driven prototyping

Small experiments that isolate risk — sensor choice, model performance, or thermal behaviour — before committing to full system designs.

Engineering transfer

Research outputs are integrated into firmware and hardware with automated tests and hardware-in-the-loop checks to maintain performance during scaling.
