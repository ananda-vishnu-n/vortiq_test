INTEGRATION

Hardware, firmware and software — designed to work together

Each product is delivered as a system: electronics and mechanical design, firmware with diagnostic interfaces, model inference where applicable, and operational tooling for updates and monitoring. Acceptance criteria and test plans are defined early and automated where possible.

System-level testing

Hardware-in-the-loop, integration test benches and field validation ensure products meet requirements under realistic conditions.

Operational tooling

Secure OTA, telemetry dashboards and diagnostic hooks to keep devices observable and maintainable.

APPLICATIONS

Where these products excel

Products are aimed at industries where robust sensing, edge inference and precise control provide clear ROI: industrial inspection, robotics, agriculture, and energy.

Industrial Inspection

Automated detection of defects, measurement and condition monitoring.

Robotics & Automation

Motion systems with deterministic control and safety interlocks.

Agricultural Systems

Sensing and edge analytics for yield estimation and health monitoring.
