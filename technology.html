OVERVIEW

A pragmatic technology stack for physical products

Vortiq focuses on the intersection of electronics, firmware, perception and control. The principal concern is delivering measurable, repeatable behavior in the environments where systems must operate — accounting for constraints such as power, latency, thermal and maintainability.

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Hardware and firmware engineered for reliability

Hardware design begins with constraints: sensor selection, analog front-ends, thermal and mechanical considerations. Firmware is structured for observability, testability and safe state transitions. We favour deterministic scheduling and clear interfaces between sensing, estimation and control.

  • Sensor signal-chain design and calibration
  • Firmware architectures with diagnostic hooks and OTA update paths
  • PCB design and manufacturability considerations

AI & COMPUTER VISION

Models engineered for edge deployment

Perception models are selected and adapted for the operational constraints they will face. Emphasis is on lightweight, robust architectures, quantization where appropriate, and validation on representative data. We integrate model outputs with system-level state estimation and safety checks.

  • Edge-friendly model architectures and pruning/quantization
  • Data collection, annotation and evaluation on realistic scenarios
  • Safety and uncertainty estimation for downstream control
ROBOTICS & CONTROL

Control systems with measurable performance

Control design emphasizes closed-loop verification and robustness to real-world disturbances. We build control stacks that combine classical control, state estimation and learned components only when they demonstrably improve performance under test conditions.

  • System identification, state estimation and observer design
  • Robust controller tuning with hardware-in-the-loop testing
  • Safety interlocks and graceful degradation strategies

CONNECTED SYSTEMS

Reliable data paths from edge to operations

Connectivity is designed for the deployment context: intermittent links, constrained bandwidth and security requirements shape telemetry, update and remote-diagnostics strategies. We design pragmatic data flows between device, gateway and cloud that enable monitoring and iterative improvement.

  • Telemetry design and diagnostic instrumentation
  • Secure, OTA update mechanisms and configuration management
  • Edge/cloud trade-offs for latency, cost and reliability
POWER & ENERGY

Designing for energy-constrained systems

Power and energy considerations are first-order constraints for many deployed systems. Design choices around sensing cadence, compute budgets, power electronics and thermal management directly affect system longevity and reliability. Vortiq engineers optimize these trade-offs with measurement-driven tests.

INTEGRATION IMAGE
ENGINEERING INTEGRATION

Bridging research to product delivery

Integration is the discipline of making components work together under operational constraints. We emphasize automated regression tests, hardware-in-the-loop validation and clear handoffs between research, firmware and systems teams so research-led improvements can become product features.
